# This file describes the reports generated from the analysis, including their purpose and how to interpret them.

## Reports Overview

The reports generated from the E-Commerce Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard project provide valuable insights into various aspects of the e-commerce business. These reports are derived from the cleaned dataset and aim to assist stakeholders in making informed decisions.

### Types of Reports

1. **Sales Performance Report**
   - **Purpose**: To analyze overall sales performance, including total revenue, total orders, and average order value.
   - **Key Metrics**: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Monthly Revenue Trends.

2. **Customer Insights Report**
   - **Purpose**: To provide insights into customer behavior and demographics.
   - **Key Metrics**: Total Customers, Revenue per Customer, Orders per Customer, Customer Distribution by Country.

3. **Product Performance Report**
   - **Purpose**: To evaluate product performance based on sales and quantity sold.
   - **Key Metrics**: Top Products by Revenue, Top Products by Quantity, Average Selling Price.

### Interpreting the Reports

- **Sales Trends**: Look for patterns in monthly revenue to identify peak sales periods.
- **Customer Behavior**: Analyze customer insights to understand purchasing patterns and identify high-value customers.
- **Product Analysis**: Use product performance metrics to determine which products are driving sales and which may need marketing support.

### Conclusion

These reports serve as a foundation for strategic decision-making in the e-commerce business, helping to identify opportunities for growth and areas for improvement.
